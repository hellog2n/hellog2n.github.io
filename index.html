My research focuses on multimodal generation and evaluation, particularly for audio and video. I am interested in developing multimodal generation and editing models, as well as benchmarks for evaluating their quality and cross-modal consistency.

  3. BMVC
    B-RIGHT: Benchmark Re-evaluation for Integrity in Generalized Human-Object Interaction Testing
    Yoojin Jang*, Junsu Kim*, Hayeon Kim, Eun-ki Lee, Eun-sol Kim, Seungryul Baek, and Jaejun Yoo
    In British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC), 2025
    Abs Bib HTML Code

    HICO-DET mixes severe class imbalance with uneven train/test counts, so HOI scores quietly inflate or deflate. B-RIGHT balances every class through automated generation-and-filtering and adds a zero-shot split — re-evaluation cuts variance and shifts rankings.

  4. ECCV
    Nickel and Diming Your GAN: A Dual-Method Approach to Enhancing GAN Efficiency via Knowledge Distillation
    Sangyeop Yeo, Yoojin Jang, and Jaejun Yoo
    In European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2024
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code

    DiME distills GANs by matching distributions in foundation-model embeddings via MMD; NICKEL keeps the student generator and discriminator talking through interactive compression. Together they reach FID 10.45 at 95.73% compression on FFHQ and still hold up at 99.69%.

  5. NeurIPS
    TopP&R: Robust Support Estimation Approach for Evaluating Fidelity and Diversity in Generative Models
    Pum Jun Kim, Yoojin Jang, Jisu Kim, and Jaejun Yoo
    Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2023
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code

    FID, IS, and precision-recall all rest on supports estimated from sample features, but nobody checks whether those supports are reliable. TopP&R keeps only topologically and statistically significant features, giving fidelity and diversity scores stable under outliers and non-IID noise.

  6. AAAI
    Can We Find Strong Lottery Tickets in Generative Models?
    Sangyeop Yeo, Yoojin Jang, Jy-yong Sohn, Dongyoon Han, and Jaejun Yoo
    In Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2023
    Oral Abs Bib HTML Code

    Oral Presentation

    Strong lottery tickets ask whether a random network already contains a working subnetwork, no weight updates allowed. We bring this to generators: only a binary mask is learned over an untrained model, guided by moment-matching scores (MMD). With 10% of weights left, it matches the dense baseline.
